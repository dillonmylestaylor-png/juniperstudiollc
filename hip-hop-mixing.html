Hip-Hop & Rap Mixing

Already have the beat and the vocals? I'll mix it for $100 a song. No studio time, no sessions to schedule — send the files from anywhere and get a finished mix back.

What you send

  • The beat — a stereo instrumental (WAV preferred)
  • Your vocals — lead, doubles, ad-libs and background vocals as separate files, recorded dry if you can
  • Optional: a reference song you want it to sit next to, and any notes

What you get

  • Vocals balanced and sitting properly on the beat
  • EQ, compression, de-essing, and cleanup on every vocal
  • Doubles, ad-libs and effects (reverb, delay) set so the hook and verses hit
  • Low end and drums checked so it translates in the car, on earbuds and on a phone speaker
  • Up to 5 rounds of revisions
  • A finished stereo mix, ready for you to release
