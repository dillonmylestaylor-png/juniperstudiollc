What it is

JS-505 starts with a real vintage analog chorus pedal. We captured the pedal's own tone, the warmth that comes from its input and output circuitry, using Neural Amp Modeler A2 (NAM A2), the same technology used to capture guitar amps. That capture runs inside the plugin, so what you hear has the character of the actual hardware, not a generic digital imitation.

The chorus itself is modeled the way the original works: a short delay that's swept slowly and blended with your dry signal, then softened the way a bucket-brigade chip softens it. Turn SPEED up for a faster shimmer, DEPTH up for a wider, wobblier sound, or keep both low for a subtle thickening that just makes a track sound bigger.

It was made and tested in a working recording studio, and it works great on guitar, bass, keys, synths and vocals. You get the sound of the pedal without the pedalboard, the cables or the hiss.

Simple on purpose

SPEED sets how fast the chorus sweeps (0.3–3 Hz). DEPTH sets how much it moves. Click the footswitch to bypass, and the LED shows when it's on.

Built on NAM A2

The tone comes from a real capture of the hardware, made with Neural Amp Modeler A2, so it has the weight and warmth of the original circuit.

Auto-Calibration

Pick your audio interface or preamp from a list of over 90 and JS-505 sets the right input level for it automatically. Or choose Line or DI and set it yourself.

Mono or stereo

Works on mono and stereo tracks, on guitar, bass, keys, synths, vocals and busses.

Frequently asked questions

What is JS-505?

JS-505 is an analog-style chorus plugin from Juniper Studio LLC. It is built from a real vintage chorus pedal, captured with Neural Amp Modeler A2 (NAM A2), and has two knobs (SPEED and DEPTH) and a footswitch.

What formats and computers does JS-505 run on?

On Mac: AU, VST3 and CLAP, for Apple Silicon and Intel, on macOS 12 or newer. On Windows: VST3 and CLAP. An AAX version for Pro Tools is coming soon.

Is JS-505 free?

You can download it and use the full plugin free for 30 days, with no account and no credit card. To keep using it, pay what you want for a license: $1 minimum, $10 suggested.

How do license keys work?

After you pay, your license key is emailed to you right away. Paste it into the plugin's Settings and click Activate. One key works on up to 10 of your machines, and you can deactivate a machine to move the license to a new computer.

Does JS-505 work on guitar, bass, keys and vocals?

Yes. It works on mono and stereo tracks, and on guitar, bass, keys, synths, vocals and busses. Auto-Calibration sets the right input level for your audio interface or preamp.

What is NAM A2?

Neural Amp Modeler (NAM), created by Steven Atkinson, is an open-source technology for capturing the sound of real analog gear. JS-505 uses its A2 models, so the tone comes from a capture of the actual hardware.
