What it is

JS-505 starts with a real vintage analog chorus pedal. The pedal's own tone, the warmth that comes from its input and output circuitry, comes from Neural Amp Modeler A2 (NAM A2), the same technology used to capture guitar amps, so what you hear has the character of the actual hardware, not a generic digital imitation.

The chorus itself is schematic-based DSP that works the way the original does: a short delay that's swept slowly and blended with your dry signal, then softened the way a bucket-brigade chip softens it. Turn SPEED up for a faster shimmer, DEPTH up for a wider, wobblier sound, or keep both low for a subtle thickening that just makes a track sound bigger.

It was made and tested in a working recording studio, and it works great on guitar, bass, keys, synths and vocals. You get the sound of the pedal without the pedalboard, the cables or the hiss.

Two knobs

SPEED (0.3–3 Hz) sets how fast the chorus sweeps. DEPTH sets how far it moves, from a gentle thickening to a deep warble. Both start at noon.

Built on NAM A2

Neural Amp Modeler A2 for the tone, schematic-based DSP for the chorus, so it has the weight and warmth of the original circuit.

Buffered Bypass

By default the footswitch is a clean bypass, linked to your DAW's. Turn on Buffered Bypass and it gives you the pedal's own buffered sound instead, like the hardware.

Auto-Calibration

Pick your audio interface or preamp from a list of over 90 and JS-505 drives the pedal's tone at the right level. Works on mono and stereo tracks, and takes mono tracks to stereo.

Frequently asked questions

What is JS-505?

JS-505 is an analog-style chorus plugin from Juniper Studio LLC. It is built from a real vintage chorus pedal, with Neural Amp Modeler A2 (NAM A2) for the tone and schematic-based DSP for the chorus, and has two knobs (SPEED and DEPTH) and a footswitch.

What formats and computers does JS-505 run on?

On Mac: AU, VST3 and CLAP, for Apple Silicon and Intel, on macOS 12 or newer. On Windows: VST3 and CLAP. An AAX version for Pro Tools is coming soon.

Is JS-505 free?

You can download it and use the full plugin free for 30 days, with no account and no credit card. To keep using it, pay what you want for a license: $1 minimum, $10 suggested.

How do license keys work?

After you pay, your license key is emailed to you right away. Paste it into the plugin's Settings and click Activate. One key works on up to 10 of your machines, and you can deactivate a machine to move the license to a new computer.

Does JS-505 work on guitar, bass, keys and vocals?

Yes. It works on mono and stereo tracks, and on guitar, bass, keys, synths, vocals and busses. Auto-Calibration sets the right input level for your audio interface or preamp.

What do the SPEED and DEPTH knobs do?

SPEED sets how fast the chorus sweeps, from a slow 0.3 Hz drift to a quick 3 Hz shimmer. DEPTH sets how far the sweep moves: low settings gently thicken and double, high settings give a deep, seasick warble. Three factory presets (Subtle Double, Classic Chorus, Deep Warble) get you started.

What is Buffered Bypass?

The real pedal doesn't just disconnect when you switch it off: your signal still passes through its buffer, which has a sound of its own. Turn on Buffered Bypass in Settings and the footswitch gives you that buffered sound, while your DAW's bypass button still bypasses the plugin completely.

What is NAM A2?

Neural Amp Modeler (NAM), created by Steven Atkinson, is an open-source technology for capturing the sound of real analog gear. JS-505 uses its A2 models for the pedal's tone, so it has the character of the actual hardware. The chorus itself is schematic-based DSP.
