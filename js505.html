JS-505

Analog-style chorus

Analog-style chorus from the same room. Try it free for 30 days, then pay what you want for a license. $1 minimum, $10 suggested.

Download for Mac — free trial Get a license (pay what you want)

Mac: AU, VST3 and CLAP, macOS 12 or newer. Windows version coming soon.

1. Download and try it

Run the installer and open JS-505 in your DAW. The full plugin works for 30 days, no account and no credit card.

2. Pay what you want

Pick any amount from $1 to $1,000. Your license key is emailed to you right after payment.

3. Paste your key

Open the settings page in the plugin, paste the key and hit Activate. One key covers up to 10 of your machines, Mac and Windows.

Moving to a new computer? Open the plugin settings and hit Deactivate on the old one first, and that slot is yours again. Trouble with a key? Email Info@JuniperStudioLLC.com.
