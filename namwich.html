What's inside

Up to 16 blocks

NAM captures and WAV or AIFF impulse responses in one chain. A noise gate is a block too.

Series, parallel, branching

Run blocks one after another, side by side, or split a path and keep one side going through more blocks before the paths meet.

Drag to reorder

Pick a block up and drop it where you want it. Double-click to load a capture, right-click to add a block.

Control every block

Bypass, mute, polarity, pan, mix and level on each one, plus bass, middle and treble, and a sample delay to line blocks up in a parallel group.

Impulse responses, your way

Choose zero-latency or normal convolution, and set the level each impulse is normalized to.

Smaller models, lighter CPU

Models that support it get a Size control, from the smallest capture in the file to the full model.

Auto-Calibration

Sets the right input level for your audio interface or preamp, so captures hit at the level they were made for.

Presets, library and tuner

Save and load whole chains, keep your models and IRs in folders inside NAMwich, and tune up without leaving the plugin.

Frequently asked questions

What is NAMwich?

NAMwich is a NAM player plugin from Juniper Studio LLC. It loads Neural Amp Modeler captures and cabinet impulse responses, up to 16 at a time, runs them in series or in parallel, and has our effect pedals built in.

What can I load into it?

NAM captures (.nam files) and impulse responses (WAV or AIFF). Each one is a block in the chain, up to 16 blocks, and you drag blocks onto each other to change the order. A noise gate is available as a block too.

How do series and parallel work?

Each block runs into the next one, or in parallel with the previous block so the two share an input and their outputs are mixed. A block can also continue the previous block's path, so one side of a split can run through more blocks before the paths meet again.

Which effects are built in?

The JS-505 chorus and JS-2 tremolo are built in at launch. Each effect gets its tone from Neural Amp Modeler A2 and its effect from schematic-based DSP, the same way the standalone pedals work.

Will new effects cost extra?

No. Every effect pedal we release later is added to NAMwich as a free update, so the plugin keeps growing and there is nothing else to buy.

How is NAMwich different from other NAM players?

Most NAM players load your captures and stop there. NAMwich does that too, and it also has real effect pedals built in, made the same hybrid way as our standalone plugins: Neural Amp Modeler A2 for the tone and schematic-based DSP for the effect.

What formats and computers does NAMwich run on?

On Mac: AU, VST3 and a standalone app, on macOS 12 or newer. On Windows: VST3. It works with mono and stereo tracks.

I own some of your effects. Do I get a discount?

How do license keys work?

After you pay, your license key is emailed to you right away. Paste it into NAMwich's Settings and click Activate. One key works on up to 10 of your machines, and you can deactivate a machine to move the license to a new computer.

Does it come with any captures?

Yes, a starter set of captures from the studio's own gear: vintage amps, pedals, and a 4x12 cabinet as both an impulse response and a NAM capture, so you can start building rigs right away.

What is NAM A2?

Neural Amp Modeler (NAM), created by Steven Atkinson, is an open-source technology for capturing the sound of real analog gear. NAMwich runs NAM captures, and its built-in effects use A2 models for their tone so they have the character of the actual hardware.
