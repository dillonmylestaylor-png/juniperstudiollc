Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to record a song at Juniper Studio?

A fully produced song — recording, mixing, and mastering — is $750 through our Production package. If you just need studio time to track, Recording alone is a 2-hour minimum session for $100 (then $50/hr after), but that covers recording only — mixing ($200/song) and mastering ($75/song) are billed separately if you book them on their own.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. We regularly run sales around holidays and other occasions throughout the year. Contact us to ask about current specials, or check our socials for announcements.

Do I need to bring my own instruments or gear?

No. Juniper Studio has a full in-house backline — custom shop guitars, vintage amps, a Steinway upright piano, and a complete drum kit — plus a high-end signal chain (API, BAE, Neumann, Royer, Coles, AEA). Artists can walk in with just their songs.

Where is Juniper Studio located and who do you work with?

The studio is based in Central City, AR, near Fort Smith. We regularly work with artists from Fayetteville, Rogers, Bentonville, Little Rock, Conway, and Russellville, AR, plus Tulsa and OKC, OK, and Nashville, TN. Remote mixing and mastering is available anywhere.

What's included in a mixing session?

Mixing is $200/song and includes a full mix from your multitracks (stems accepted if that's all you have) — EQ, compression, automation, spatial processing, and reference mastering — with up to 5 rounds of revisions.

Do you offer discounts for a full album or EP?

Yes. Album packages bundle recording, mixing, mastering, and project management at a discount: 3 songs for $1,500, 5 songs for $2,000, or 10 songs for $3,000, versus paying for each service separately.
