About the studio

Juniper Studio was founded with one goal: to give musicians a space where technology and artistry meet. We combine a world-class signal chain (API, BAE, Purple Audio, Fairchild, Neumann, Royer, Coles, AEA), a full guitar/amp/keys/drums backline, and a comfortable creative environment.
