About the studio

Juniper Studio was built so musicians have a space where technology and artistry meet — a world-class signal chain (API, BAE, Purple Audio, Fairchild, Neumann, Royer, Coles, AEA), a full guitar/amp/keys/drums backline, and a comfortable creative room.

🎧

Track & Record

Pristine recording chain with vintage and boutique outboard preamps, high-end ribbon and condenser microphones, and a treated live room.

Who you'll work with

Sessions are engineered by Dillon Taylor — almost 20 years in the business, hundreds of songs, and a multi-instrumentalist (guitar most of all). You work one-on-one with the person mixing and producing your record, not a rotating intern. Need a part tracked? That's in-house too.
